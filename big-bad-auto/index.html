Our Story

MEET ALEX
& JOSE

Big Bad Auto was born from a simple frustration: finding a mechanic you can actually trust. Alex and his father Jose founded this shop because they believed San Marcos deserved an auto repair experience that puts the customer first — every single time.

As a father-and-son team, they bring combined decades of hands-on experience and a level of personal accountability you simply won't find at a dealership or chain shop. When you call Big Bad Auto, you're talking to an owner.

Their rule is non-negotiable: nothing gets touched without your approval. No surprise charges. No work done without a conversation first. Just expert diagnosis, honest pricing, and repairs you can feel good about.
