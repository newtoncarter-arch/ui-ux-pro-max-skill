Our Services

Individualized, evidence-based support delivered in-home, in the community, or via telehealth — wherever it's most meaningful for you.

Neuroaffirming & Inclusive Services

While not all of our clients have ADHD or are autistic, we support many who are. Our neuroaffirming services respect and celebrate neurodiversity while addressing the real challenges individuals face. We specialize in executive function challenges common for neurodivergent individuals, always centering the person's strengths, identity, and autonomy.

Service 1 of 4

Executive Functioning Tutoring

Our executive functioning coaching focuses on building the mental skills people use every day to get things done. We work with individuals to develop practical systems and routines that increase independence and reduce daily stress in school, work, and home settings.

Sessions are practical and skill-based, using behavior skills training and systematic prompt fading to teach strategies in the environments where they're most needed. Goals are always individualized and driven by what matters most to the client.

Service 2 of 4

Social Skills & Relationship Development

Meaningful friendships, workplace relationships, and romantic connections are central to a fulfilling life. We teach the "soft skills" that help individuals navigate social situations with confidence — from starting a conversation to maintaining a long-term friendship.

We offer the evidence-based UCLA PEERS® program as well as individualized coaching tailored to each person's specific social goals. Coaching is available individually or in small groups, and includes parent/caregiver coaching when appropriate.

Service 3 of 4

Independence in Community, Career & Life Skills

Our life skills services are designed to help individuals build greater independence through the use of behavior skills training and systematic prompt fading. We teach adaptive and functional skills in real-world settings — home, school, work, and the community.

We focus on career readiness and job coaching, helping individuals prepare for and succeed in work environments. Community integration is a core goal: using public transportation, shopping, navigating community resources, and participating in leisure activities are all within scope.

Service 4 of 4

Family & Caregiver Consultation for Behavior Support

Families and caregivers are crucial partners in the success of the individuals we support. We offer consultation and coaching to help families understand and implement strategies at home, and to ensure consistency across environments.

We also provide team training and supervision for Independent Facilitators and support staff, and can develop individualized behavior support plans when needed. All behavior support is grounded in compassion and respect for the individual.
