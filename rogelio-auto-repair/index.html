What We Do

Full-Service Auto Repair
You Can Depend On

From routine maintenance to complex repairs, Rogelio's team handles it all with care and expertise.

Brake Repair & Replacement

Pads, rotors, calipers, brake fluid flush — we diagnose and fix all brake system issues to keep you stopping safely.

Oil Changes

Conventional, synthetic, and high-mileage oil changes done right — quick turnaround to get you back on the road.

Transmission Service

Fluid service, filter replacement, diagnostics, and repair for automatic and manual transmissions.

Electrical Repairs

Starters, alternators, batteries, wiring issues, and check-engine diagnostics. We track down electrical gremlins fast.

Mechanical Repairs

Suspension, cooling systems, belts, hoses, steering, and general engine work. No job too big or too small.

Smog Checks

Certified smog testing for California DMV registration renewal. In and out fast — we're an official CA DMV partner.
