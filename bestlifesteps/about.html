Our Story

About Best Life Steps, LLC

Best Life Steps, LLC was founded with a mission to support individuals in building independence and living lives that bring a sense of happiness, purpose, and connection. Our neuroaffirming services are grounded in evidence-based practices and delivered with kindness, compassion, and deep respect for each person's unique strengths, needs, and goals.

We specialize in supporting adolescents and adults with and without disabilities, including individuals with autism and related challenges. Our primary focus is on developing executive functioning, social, and life skills that promote independence, confidence, and quality of life.

Services are individualized and practical, helping clients apply skills in real-world settings such as home, school, work, and the community.

Our executive functioning coaching focuses on skills such as planning, organization, time management, task initiation, problem solving, flexibility, impulse control, working memory, self-monitoring, and emotional regulation. We support clients in building systems and routines that increase independence and reduce daily stress.

We also offer social skills and relationship development, including the UCLA PEERS® program, as well as individualized coaching to develop communication, conversation, and relationship-building skills — including friendships, dating, and workplace relationships.

Additional services include family consultation and caregiver coaching, as well as team training and supervision. Best Life Steps, LLC primarily serves North County San Diego and surrounding areas, with services provided in-home, in the community, or via telehealth when appropriate.
