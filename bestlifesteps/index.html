What We Offer

Our Services

Individualized services designed to meet each person where they are — and support them in getting where they want to go.

Executive Functioning Tutoring

Planning, organization, time management, task initiation, problem-solving, flexibility, impulse control, working memory, self-monitoring, and emotional regulation — we help build systems and routines that increase independence.

Learn more

Social Skills & Relationship Development

Including the evidence-based UCLA PEERS® program, we support communication, conversation, friendship-building, and navigating social situations — helping individuals form meaningful connections.

Learn more

Independence in Community, Career & Life Skills

Daily living routines, community participation, career readiness, job coaching, leisure & recreation, health and wellness — practical skills taught in real-world settings where they matter most.

Learn more

Family & Caregiver Consultation

Coaching and consultation for families and caregivers, team training, supervision, and individualized behavior support plans for individuals with mild to moderate behavior challenges.

Learn more

Our Approach

Why Families Choose Best Life Steps

Our work is guided by four core commitments that shape every session and every relationship.

Personalized Approach

Every plan is tailored to the individual's unique goals, values, and aspirations — never a one-size-fits-all program.

Evidence-Based Practices

Our methods are grounded in the latest research, ensuring effective strategies that promote lasting behavioral change.

Compassionate Support

Every interaction is infused with kindness and respect, creating a safe environment for individuals to grow and thrive.

Community Integration

We focus on helping individuals participate fully in their community, enhancing social connections and real-world opportunities.
